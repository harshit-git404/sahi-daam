# Sahi Daam — System Architecture Specification

## 1. Existing System vs. New Invention Architecture

### Legacy Prototype Architecture (Linear / Feed-Forward)
The original Sahi Daam prototype implemented a single-pass, feed-forward workflow:
1. **Input Observation**: The user captured a single image of a commodity.
2. **Produce Identification & Freshness**: An external vision model or local classifier predicted commodity type and a freshness percentage.
3. **Point Price Lookup**: A single wholesale price was obtained from an Agmarknet endpoint (or fell back to hardcoded defaults).
4. **Heuristic Deductions**: Rigid flat adjustments (e.g., `-₹2/kg` for slightly aged, `-₹5/kg` for overripe) were subtracted.
5. **Deterministic Interval**: A static percentage markup was added to generate a retail price range.
6. **Binary Deal Classification**: A fixed asking price was labeled `OVERPRICED`, `FAIR`, or `GOOD_DEAL` regardless of whether the visual evidence or market evidence was trustworthy.

### The New Invention: Closed-Loop Uncertainty-Driven Adaptive Valuation System
The transformed architecture treats commodity pricing as an **active perception and information-theoretic fusion problem** under epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty:

```
[Observation 1] 
     ↓
[Quality Estimation & Disagreement]
     ↓
[Mathematical Uncertainty Estimation]
     ↓
[Multi-Source Market Ingestion]
     ↓
[Source Reliability Scoring & Market Dispersion]
     ↓
[Quality-Price Adjustment Model]
     ↓
[Compound Uncertainty Propagation]
     ↓
[Adaptive Observation Controller (EVI vs. Cost)]
  ├── If EVI > Cost: Formulate Targeted Request ("Capture opposite side") ──┐
  │                                                                         │
  │   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  │   ↓
  │ [User Captures Observation 2] → [Multi-Observation Fusion Engine] ──→ Recompute Loop
  │
  └── If EVI <= Cost / Confident: Finalize Valuation
            ↓
      [Uncertainty-Gated Anomaly Classification]
            ↓
      [Confidence-Aware Tactical Negotiation Strategy]
```

## 2. Core Architectural Modules

The refactored system is partitioned into strictly decoupled, model-agnostic components located in `backend/`:

### A. Core Schema Layer (`backend/core/schemas.py`)
Defines immutable, strongly-typed data structures:
- `Observation`: Multi-view records holding image data, camera metadata, viewpoints (`primary`, `reverse_side`, `close_up`), and quality metrics.
- `ObservationRequest`: Formal request generated by the adaptive controller specifying reason, target angle, user guidance, priority, and expected information gain.
- `QualityEstimate`: Commodity identity, quality score ($[0,1]$), freshness score ($[0,1]$), quality class, confidence, uncertainty, and defect notes.
- `MarketEvidenceRecord`: Individual market evidence from mandis or caches with timestamp, geographic distance, authority weight, price per unit, and source reliability.
- `WeightedMarketPriceEstimate`: Normalized reliability-weighted wholesale price, sample variance, source dispersion, and composite market uncertainty.
- `ValuationResult`: Fair price interval $[\text{FairMin}, \text{FairMax}]$, mid-price, compound variance $\sigma_{\text{fair}}^2$, valuation uncertainty, and component traces.
- `ControllerDecision`: Decision enum (`ACQUIRE_ADDITIONAL_OBSERVATION` vs. `FINALIZE_VALUATION`), EVI value, observation cost, and rationale.
- `ValuationSession`: Stateful session tracking sequential observations, incremental estimates, step-by-step decision traces, and before/after comparison metrics.

### B. Uncertainty Estimation Engine (`backend/uncertainty/estimator.py`)
Computes an epistemic quality uncertainty metric $U_{\text{quality}} \in [0, 1]$ from measurable visual and model signals without arbitrary random numbers:
$$U_{\text{quality}} = \min\left(1.0, w_c (1 - C) + w_d D + w_i P_{\text{image}} + w_v P_{\text{coverage}} + w_a P_{\text{ambiguity}}\right)$$
Where:
- $C$: Classifier confidence score.
- $D$: Model disagreement between multimodal visual estimators.
- $P_{\text{image}}$: Image blur/lighting penalty.
- $P_{\text{coverage}}$: Incomplete visual coverage penalty (e.g., single planar view of spherical produce).
- $P_{\text{ambiguity}}$: Margin between top-1 and top-2 candidate classifications.

### C. Model-Agnostic Quality Estimators (`backend/quality/estimator.py`)
Implements the `IQualityEstimator` interface:
- `GeminiQualityEstimator`: Cloud multimodal VLM implementation.
- `LocalQualityEstimator`: Local PyTorch/Keras computer vision pipeline.
- `HybridQualityEstimator`: Production hybrid that queries primary and secondary estimators, measures inter-model divergence $D = |S_1 - S_2|$, and computes compound uncertainty.
- `SimulatedQualityEstimator`: Deterministic scenario generator for unit testing, reproducible benchmark validation, and field demonstrations.

### D. Multi-Source Market Evidence Provider & Reliability Scorer (`backend/market/evidence.py`, `backend/market/reliability.py`)
Ingests heterogeneous market records:
- Live local APMC mandi reports (Agmarknet).
- Neighboring district mandis within spatial proximity.
- Temporal history (previous-day / 3-day regional averages).
- Validated local cache records.

Each record is evaluated by the **Market Source Reliability Scorer**:
$$\text{Reliability} = w_{\text{recency}} S_{\text{recency}} + w_{\text{geo}} S_{\text{geo}} + w_{\text{source}} S_{\text{source}} + w_{\text{consistency}} S_{\text{consistency}}$$
- **Recency Decay**: Exponential half-life decay based on timestamp staleness.
- **Geographic Proximity**: Distance-weighted penalty ($e^{-d / d_0}$).
- **Source Authority**: Mandi official tiering vs. crowdsourced or cached streams.
- **Group Consistency**: Distance of price $P_i$ from the median of all co-temporal observations.

### E. Weighted Market Price Estimator (`backend/market/estimator.py`)
Synthesizes market evidence into a weighted wholesale estimate:
$$P_{\text{market}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^N w_i \cdot P_i}{\sum_{i=1}^N w_i}, \quad w_i = \text{Reliability}_i$$
Computes market dispersion uncertainty:
$$\sigma_{\text{market\_sources}}^2 = \sum_{i=1}^N w_i \cdot (P_i - P_{\text{market}})^2$$
$$U_{\text{market}} = \text{clip}\left(0.4 \cdot U_{\text{dispersion}} + 0.35 \cdot U_{\text{staleness}} + 0.25 \cdot U_{\text{sparsity}}, 0.05, 0.95\right)$$

### F. Quality-to-Price Adjustment Model (`backend/valuation/adjustment.py`)
Replaces rigid flat deductions with a dynamic, market-relative, perishability-aware function:
$$\Delta P_{\text{quality}} = P_{\text{market}} \cdot \beta_{\text{commodity}} \cdot (Q - Q_{\text{baseline}})$$
Where $\beta_{\text{commodity}}$ represents the perishability sensitivity index (e.g., higher for tomatoes/greens, lower for potatoes/onions), and $Q$ is the fused quality score.

### G. Uncertainty-Aware Valuation Engine (`backend/valuation/engine.py`)
Synthesizes the retail price range while strictly propagating component variances:
$$\sigma_{\text{fair}}^2 = \sigma_{\text{market}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{adjustment}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{model}}^2$$
The fair price interval is dynamically scaled by compound uncertainty:
$$\Delta = Z \cdot \sigma_{\text{fair}} \cdot \left(1.0 + \lambda \cdot U_{\text{valuation}}\right)$$
$$\text{FairMin} = \max\left(1.0, P_{\text{fair\_mid}} - \Delta\right), \quad \text{FairMax} = P_{\text{fair\_mid}} + \Delta$$
Under high uncertainty, the interval $[\text{FairMin}, \text{FairMax}]$ widens honestly; as additional observations reduce uncertainty, the interval narrows.

### H. Adaptive Observation Controller (`backend/controller/observation_controller.py`)
Evaluates the **Expected Value of Information (EVI)** against observation cost:
$$\text{Expected Uncertainty Reduction} = U_{\text{current}} \cdot \gamma_{\text{gain}}$$
$$\text{EVI} = \text{Expected Uncertainty Reduction} - \text{Cost}_{\text{observation}}$$
If $\text{EVI} > \tau_{\text{threshold}}$ and $k < K_{\text{max}}$, the controller outputs `ACQUIRE_ADDITIONAL_OBSERVATION` and crafts a targeted `ObservationRequest`. Otherwise, it finalizes the valuation.

### I. Multi-Observation Fusion Engine (`backend/observation/fusion.py`)
Fuses sequential observations:
- **Defect Preservation Rule**: If View 1 indicates high freshness (0.90) but View 2 detects severe bruising or decay (0.35), the engine does not perform a simple arithmetic average that masks decay. It applies defect weighting to ensure visible damage lowers the final quality score.
- **Uncertainty Reduction**: When independent viewpoints agree on quality parameters, inter-view consensus reduces visual coverage uncertainty:
  $$U_{\text{fused}} = \frac{U_{\text{initial}}}{\sqrt{N_{\text{agreeing}}}} \cdot \left(1 + \text{DisagreementPenalty}\right)$$

### J. Price-Quality Anomaly Detector (`backend/pricing/anomaly.py`)
Gated by valuation confidence. When valuation uncertainty is high ($U > 0.60$ or $C < 0.40$), the system rejects making an assertive `OVERPRICED` accusation against the vendor and instead issues `INSUFFICIENT_CONFIDENCE`, preventing unjust bargaining friction.

## 3. Data Flow and Session Lifecycle

1. **Session Creation**: Client triggers `POST /valuation/session` with initial image and commodity name.
2. **Session Initial Valuation**: Quality estimated $\rightarrow$ Market evidence scored $\rightarrow$ Initial fair interval calculated $\rightarrow$ Controller determines if View 2 is needed.
3. **Adaptive Feedback Loop**: If View 2 is needed, the client displays specific guidance ("Capture opposite side"). The user captures a secondary angle and calls `POST /valuation/observe`.
4. **Evidence Re-synthesis**: Fusion engine combines observations $\rightarrow$ Valuation engine recalculates compound variance $\rightarrow$ Controller finalizes.
5. **Downstream Negotiation**: Final fair interval and confidence score guide the tactical negotiation script in `POST /haggle-check`.
